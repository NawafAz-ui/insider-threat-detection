Benchmark Evidence

This page backs every cell of Table I in the Capstone report (Section V-B, Benchmarking with other systems). Use it to check a cell or answer a question about it.

Checked on 16 November 2026 against each system's own documentation. Quotes are shortened where marked with ... . Yes: stated. Partly: limited, optional or an add-on. No: not offered or commercial only. n/s: not stated.

What to check before submitting

  • Some quotes came through a page reader, not the raw page: Proofpoint P1, P4, P5, P6 and P9, the Exabeam pages and the Securonix web pages. Open them once in a browser.
  • Exabeam's session score comes from its older Advanced Analytics product. The new New-Scale platform scores threats, not sessions.
  • Splunk UBA: end of sale announced in December 2025 (the FAQ says 10 December, the help page 12 December), end of life 31 January 2027.
  • DTEX technical documentation needs a customer login, so its rows rest on product pages. Securonix rows rest on product pages and datasheets.
  • The Proofpoint baseline answer rests on a 2020 brief, and the Wazuh machine-learning answer on a 2023 blog post.
